(About)

Transforming Real Estate with
Global Excellence

Experion is an FDI-backed real estate developer and a subsidiary of Experion Holding Pte. Limited, Singapore. Since entering India in 2006, it has delivered 10 landmark projects across Gurugram, Lucknow, Noida, and Amritsar, covering over 10.9 million sq. ft. of developed space.

Experion Group has a strong presence across multiple Indian states and is developing integrated townships, luxury residences, commercial spaces, hotels, and resorts.
